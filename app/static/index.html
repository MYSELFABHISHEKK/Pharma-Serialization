    Technical Knowledge Hub

    Frequently Asked Questions on Pharmaceutical Serialization.

    Standard operating guidance for packaging supervisors, QA compliance officers, and validation engineers.

    ❓ How does the portal prevent duplicate serial numbers across high-speed packaging lines?

    The serialization engine combines atomic database sequence reservation (using row-level locking on sequence counters) with an OS-level Cryptographically Secure Pseudo-Random Number Generator (CSPRNG). Candidates are cross-referenced across historical chunks, mathematically ensuring zero duplicate allocations.

    ❓ What is the difference between SGTIN-96, EPCIS, and 2D DataMatrix?

    GS1 2D DataMatrix is the physical two-dimensional barcode printed on the carton. SGTIN-96 is the 96-bit global unique identifier combining GTIN and Serial Number. EPCIS (Electronic Product Code Information Services) is the XML/JSON standard for transmitting supply-chain events between trading partners.

    ❓ Why are look-alike characters (0, 1, I, L, O, U) excluded from serialization?

    In accordance with GS1 General Specifications and global health authority recommendations, visually ambiguous characters are excluded from alphanumeric serialization to prevent Optical Character Recognition (OCR) misreads and human scanning confusion during clinical point-of-dispense.

    ❓ How does the portal support 21 CFR Part 11 and EU GMP Annex 11 inspections?

    All database mutations (creates, updates, and status transitions) generate an immutable Change Data Capture (CDC) audit log containing UTC timestamps, actor usernames, and complete before-and-after JSON snapshots that cannot be modified or truncated by users.
