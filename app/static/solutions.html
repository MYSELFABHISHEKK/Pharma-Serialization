1. Pharmaceutical Serialization

Turnkey Level 1 through Level 4 serialization solutions generating tamper-evident serial identifiers validated against global health authority requirements.

1.1 Serialization Overview

Unit-Level Coding • GS1 Standards

Comprehensive packaging line serialization engineered for solid orals, injectables, vials, ampoules, and biologics. Generating unique, cryptographically random SGTIN-96 identifiers compliant with GS1 standards.

  • ✓ GS1 SGTIN-96 Standard: 2D DataMatrix encoding Application Identifiers (01) GTIN, (21) Serial Number, (17) Expiry Date, and (10) Batch/Lot Number.
  • ✓ CSPRNG Cryptographic Entropy: High-entropy pseudo-random serial numbers with 1018 sequence entropy ensuring zero sequence predictability.
  • ✓ High-Speed In-Line Printing: Continuous industrial thermal inkjet and laser marking up to 450 packs/minute on blister cartons and bottle labels.
  • ✓ Real-Time Packaging Verification: Synchronous optical character verification (OCV) and DataMatrix decodability validation at production speed.
